📰 **Individual Time Trial (Elite Men) – UCI Road World Championships, Montreal 2026**

The men's individual time trial at the 2026 UCI Road World Championships in Montreal delivered one of the most memorable performances in modern cycling history. The 39.2 km course, starting at Avenue du Parc and finishing at Parc Jeanne-Mance, challenged riders with technical sections and a demanding pacing strategy. 🚴‍♂️⏱️

Belgian rider **Remco Evenepoel** once again showed supreme dominance in the discipline, winning **his fourth consecutive world title**. With exceptional stability and power throughout the ride, Evenepoel stopped the clock at **44:53.13**, setting a new personal best and further solidifying his status as the strongest specialist in the individual time trial of his generation. 🥇🔥

Italian **Filippo Ganna** fought for the silver medal with the smooth, fluid style and aerodynamic efficiency that have become his trademark. **Paul Seixas** completed the podium with a performance that underscored his rapid rise among the world elite. 🥈🥉

The race was a true test of endurance, precision, and mental strength — qualities that define the best riders in the world. Montreal 2026 became one of the strongest editions of the individual time trial in recent years. 🌍💨
