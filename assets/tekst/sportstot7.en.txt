The Most Popular Sports in Indonesia

Indonesia has a rich sports culture where traditional and modern sports exist side by side. Below you can find the most widely practiced sports in the country.

1. Football (Sepak Bola) - the most popular sport
Why is it so popular?
Football is a national passion in Indonesia. Matches of the national team (Garuda) and local clubs attract huge interest. Liga 1 Indonesia is the top football division in the country.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia is the only Southeast Asian country that has played at a FIFA World Cup (1938).

Stadiums: Gelora Bung Karno (Jakarta), Gelora Bandung Lautan Api (Bandung).
How can you join?
In many cities, you can play on public pitches for free or for a small fee.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to watch a match in Indonesia, visit coffee shops (warung kopi) or local sports halls (lapangan), where people often watch football together.

2. Badminton (Bulu Tangkis) - Indonesia's national sport
Why is it so important?
Badminton is a national sport and one of Indonesia's most successful disciplines. Indonesian players such as Taufik Hidayat and Susi Susanti are world champions.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia has won Olympic gold medals in badminton at almost every Olympic Games since 1992 (except 2012).

Where can you play?
Clubs: PB Djarum, PB Tangkas (Jakarta).
Public courts: Many parks and sports centers offer courts for rent.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to learn badminton, look for youth badminton clubs that offer beginner lessons.

3. Volleyball (Bola Voli) - a sport for all ages
Why is it popular?
Volleyball is accessible and easy to practice. You will see it on beaches, in schools, and on community courts.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia also has mountain volleyball, played at higher altitudes where the air is thinner.

Where can you play?
Beaches: Kuta Beach (Bali), Pantai Losari (Makassar).
Public courts: Many city parks have volleyball courts.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to play volleyball in Indonesia, look for friendly local matches (pertandingan persahabatan).

4. Pencak Silat - traditional martial art
Why is it important?
Pencak Silat is a traditional Indonesian martial art practiced for centuries. It includes strikes, grappling, and the use of weapons such as the kris.

Interesting fact:
Pencak Silat is included in the Asian Games and is one of the world's oldest martial arts.

Where can you train?
Schools: IPSI (Ikatan Pencak Silat Indonesia) and many local sports centers.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to understand Pencak Silat better, watch documentaries and visit cultural festivals in Indonesia.

5. Cycling (Balap Sepeda) - growing popularity
Why is it popular?
Cycling is becoming more and more popular, especially among younger people. Indonesia offers many mountain and flat routes suitable for riding.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia hosts Tour de Indonesia, an international cycling event.

Where can you ride?
Mountain routes: Mount Bromo (East Java), Mount Rinjani (Lombok).
City bike lanes: Jakarta, Bandung, and Surabaya have dedicated bike lanes.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to start cycling, look for local cycling clubs or bike shops that organize rides and lessons.

6. Tennis (Tenis) - popular among the middle class
Why is it popular?
Tennis is widely available in private and public clubs, especially in large cities.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia has hosted major international tennis events and has produced notable players.

Where can you play?
Clubs: Jakarta Tennis Club, Bandung Tennis Club.
Public courts: Many city parks offer paid tennis courts.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to learn tennis, search for tennis academies or beginner classes.

7. Rowing (Dayung) - a sport with tradition
Why is it important?
Rowing is a traditional sport in Indonesia, especially in regions such as Kalimantan and Sulawesi where boats are part of daily life.

Interesting fact:
Indonesia hosts rowing festivals and regattas such as Pesta Laut.

Where can you practice?
Rivers and lakes: Lake Toba (Sumatra), Rawa Pening (Central Java).
Clubs: Jakarta Rowing Club.

Teacher Tip:
If you want to try rowing, look for rowing clubs or canoe and kayak lessons.
