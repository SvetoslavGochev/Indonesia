Complete Indonesia Travel Guide\n\nWhy Indonesia?\nIndonesia is a diverse island country with more than 17,000 islands, rich culture, tropical beaches, volcanoes, and unforgettable food.\n\nBest Time to Visit\n- Dry season (most places): April to October\n- Rainy season: November to March\n- Typical temperature: 25-32 C\n\nHow to Get There\nMain international airports:\n- Jakarta (CGK)\n- Bali / Denpasar (DPS)\n- Medan (KNO)\n\nTop Destinations\n1. Bali: beaches, temples, Ubud culture, sunsets.\n2. Java: Jakarta, Yogyakarta, Borobudur, volcano views.\n3. Komodo: famous dragons and world-class diving spots.\n4. Sumatra: rainforests, wildlife, orangutans, adventurous routes.\n5. Raja Ampat area: pristine islands and spectacular marine life.\n\nFood You Should Try\n- Nasi Goreng\n- Satay\n- Rendang\n- Gado-Gado\n- Bami Goreng\n\nSafety and Health\n- Use bottled water.\n- Keep valuables secure in busy tourist zones.\n- Use mosquito repellent.\n- Consider travel insurance and basic vaccinations.\n\nCulture and Etiquette\n- Dress modestly in temples.\n- Respect local traditions and religious customs.\n- Basic phrases are always appreciated by locals.\n\nConclusion\nIndonesia is a destination that combines nature, culture, food, and adventure. Whether you want a relaxing beach trip or an active island-hopping route, Indonesia offers a memorable experience.
