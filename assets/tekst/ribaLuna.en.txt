Title: Mola Mola (Ocean Sunfish): The Giant Wanderer of Deep Waters

Short description: Seen around Nusa Penida and other Indonesian waters, this unusual fish is famous for its flat body and massive size.

Interesting facts:

It is the heaviest bony fish on Earth and can weigh well over 1,000 kg.

It has no true tail fin and mainly feeds on jellyfish and other soft-bodied prey.

It often rises from deep colder waters to the surface to warm up and let birds and fish clean parasites from its skin.
