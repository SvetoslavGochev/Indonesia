Indonesia is home to many unique land animals. Here are 8 of the most iconic species with useful details on weight, speed, and habitat:

1. Javan Rhino (Rhinoceros sondaicus)
Weight: 900-2,300 kg
Speed: Up to 40 km/h (short sprints)
Habitat: Ujung Kulon National Park (West Java) - tropical forests and wetlands.
Interesting: One of the rarest rhinos in the world (around 70 individuals). It feeds on leaves, shoots, and fruit.
2. Sumatran Tiger (Panthera tigris sumatrae)
Weight: 90-140 kg (males), 65-90 kg (females)
Speed: Up to 65 km/h (short bursts)
Habitat: Sumatra and nearby Indonesian forest zones - tropical and mangrove forests.
Interesting: The smallest tiger subspecies. It is an excellent swimmer and mostly hunts at night.
3. Orangutan (Pongo spp.)
Weight: 50-120 kg (males), 30-50 kg (females)
Speed: 5-10 km/h on the ground, but very agile in trees
Habitat: Bornean and Sumatran rainforests.
Interesting: It uses tools and has very high intelligence. Deforestation remains its biggest threat.
4. Komodo Dragon (Varanus komodoensis)
Weight: 70-90 kg
Speed: Up to 20 km/h (short runs)
Habitat: Komodo, Rinca, and Flores islands - dry savannas and tropical woodland.
Interesting: The largest living lizard species. Its bite can cause rapid blood loss and severe infection.
5. Sumatran Elephant (Elephas maximus sumatranus)
Weight: 2,000-4,000 kg
Speed: 15-20 km/h
Habitat: Forest areas in Sumatra and Borneo.
Interesting: A smaller Asian elephant subspecies under pressure from habitat loss and poaching.
6. Babirusa (Babyrousa babyrussa)
Weight: 60-100 kg
Speed: Up to 40 km/h
Habitat: Sulawesi and nearby islands - tropical forests and marshy zones.
Interesting: Famous for its curved tusks that grow upward and can even pierce the skull in old males.
7. Sun Bear (Helarctos malayanus)
Weight: 25-80 kg
Speed: 25-35 km/h (short bursts)
Habitat: Tropical forests of Sumatra and Borneo.
Interesting: The smallest bear species in the world. It has a pale chest patch and is a highly agile climber.
8. Anoa (Bubalus depressicornis)
Weight: 150-300 kg
Speed: 20-30 km/h
Habitat: Dense forests and hills of Sulawesi.
Interesting: This small wild buffalo is endemic to Indonesia and is closely tied to the island's terrestrial fauna.
