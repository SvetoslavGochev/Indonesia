Title: Blue-ringed Octopus: Tiny Beauty, Serious Warning

Short description: Found in the Indo-Pacific, including Indonesian reefs, this small octopus is admired for its electric blue rings that appear when it feels threatened.

Interesting facts:

Despite being only about the size of a golf ball, it carries a highly potent venom called tetrodotoxin.

Its blue rings pulse and become bright as a warning signal when it senses danger.

It is a very intelligent animal and a master of camouflage and hunting among coral reef crevices.
