TOP 5 INDONESIAN CRUISE DESTINATIONS

1. BALI (Denpasar, Benoa)
- Why visit: Famous beaches, temples, tropical landscapes
- Best period: April-October (dry season)

2. KOMODO ISLAND (Labuan Bajo)
- Why visit: Home of Komodo dragons and excellent diving spots
- Best period: May-September

3. JAVA (Jakarta, Semarang, Surabaya)
- Why visit: Volcanoes, historic cities, Borobudur temple
- Best period: May-October

4. LOMBOK (Mataram)
- Why visit: Quieter alternative to Bali, waterfalls, diving
- Best period: April-October

5. RAJA AMPAT
- Why visit: One of the best diving regions in the world
- Best period: October-April

PRICE GUIDE (2024)

- Standard cruise options: around $900 to $2,500 per person for 7 days
- Luxury cruise options: around $2,800 to $6,000 per person for 7 days
- Usually included: accommodation, basic meals, onboard entertainment

Tips before booking:
- Book 3-6 months early for better prices
- Midweek departures are often cheaper
- Add travel insurance
- Check visa requirements for each port
- Ask for special packages for families or honeymoon trips
