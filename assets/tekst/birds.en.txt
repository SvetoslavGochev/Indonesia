1. Javan Ostrich (Rhea americana)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

One of the largest flightless birds and a very fast runner.
It uses powerful legs for defense and movement in open terrain.
Feeds on plants, seeds, and small invertebrates.
⚖️ Weight: about 20-40 kg | 🏃 Speed: up to about 60 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In savannas and open grasslands.

2. Cockatoo (Cacatua)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

One of the most intelligent and social birds.
It has a distinctive crest and loud voice; some species imitate sounds.
Lives in groups and maintains complex flock communication.
⚖️ Weight: about 0.3-1.2 kg | 🏃 Speed: up to about 45 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In forests and suburban areas in tropical and subtropical zones.

3. Harpy Eagle (Harpyopsis novaeguineae)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

A large bird of prey with powerful claws and excellent vision.
Hunts small and medium animals in dense forests.
Often perches high in trees to observe its territory.
⚖️ Weight: about 2-3.5 kg | 🏃 Speed: flight up to about 50 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In tropical forests and mountain regions.

4. Hornbill (Bucerotidae)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

Recognizable by its large curved bill and unique profile.
The bill is used for feeding, defense, and courtship display.
Feeds on fruit, insects, and small animals.
⚖️ Weight: about 1-4 kg | 🏃 Speed: flight up to about 40 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In tropical forests, including Indonesia.

5. Bird-of-paradise (Paradisaeidae)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

Considered one of the most beautiful birds in the world.
Males have bright, elongated feathers and perform impressive mating dances.
Prefers humid forests with rich biodiversity.
⚖️ Weight: about 0.05-0.45 kg | 🏃 Speed: flight up to about 35 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In tropical forests of New Guinea and nearby islands.

6. Green Parrot (Psittacula)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

A small but energetic and noisy parrot.
Has a strong beak used to crack seeds, nuts, and fruit.
Adapts well to both forest and urban environments.
⚖️ Weight: about 0.1-0.2 kg | 🏃 Speed: flight up to about 50 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In forests, parks, and urban green zones.

7. Javan Pheasant (Pavo muticus)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

A bird with striking bright blue-green plumage.
Males spread their long feathers during mating rituals.
Prefers open forest and grassland habitats.
⚖️ Weight: about 3.5-5 kg | 🏃 Speed: running up to about 25 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In Southeast Asia, including Java island.

8. Myna (Acridotheres)
🔹 Why is it remarkable?

Known for imitating human speech and urban sounds.
Highly adaptable and easily finds food near people.
Feeds on insects, fruit, and small seeds.
⚖️ Weight: about 0.08-0.14 kg | 🏃 Speed: flight up to about 40 km/h.
🌍 Where is it found? In cities, parks, and agricultural regions.
