Top 3 easy summer dishes from Indonesia to refresh your day!

Introduction:

Summer is the perfect season for light, quick, and delicious meals.
Indonesian cuisine offers great options for hot days with aromatic and refreshing flavors.
Today we share 3 easy recipes that can impress everyone!
Top 3 dishes:

Gado-Gado (Indonesian salad with peanut sauce)
Why it is great for summer: Light, refreshing, and rich in vitamins.
Ingredients: Vegetables (cabbage, cucumber, carrots), hard-boiled eggs, tofu, peanuts, coconut milk.
Steps:
Chop the vegetables finely.
Boil the eggs and cut them into pieces.
Prepare a peanut sauce with coconut milk, lemon juice, garlic, and a little chili.
Mix everything together and serve with the sauce.
Sate Lilit (Indonesian fish or meat skewers)
Why it is great for summer: Quick to prepare and easy to serve outdoors.
Ingredients: Fish or chicken, coconut milk, onion, garlic, ginger, lemon juice.
Steps:
Mix all ingredients and blend into a paste.
Spread the mixture onto skewers or sticks.
Grill or bake until fully cooked.
Es Campur (Indonesian refreshing dessert)
Why it is great for summer: Icy, sweet, and refreshing.
Ingredients: Coconut milk, mixed fruits (pineapple, mango, quince), gelatin, ice.
Steps:
Prepare jelly with gelatin and water.
Cut the fruits into pieces.
Mix everything in a glass with ice and coconut milk.
Conclusion:

These dishes are quick, tasty, and refreshing.
