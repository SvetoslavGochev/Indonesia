Discover the beauty of Lombok: a guide to your island adventure

1. Mount Rinjani
Lombok's most famous landmark is Mount Rinjani, an active volcano with dramatic views, crater lakes, and trekking routes for adventurous travelers. If you plan to hike, go with a licensed guide and check the weather first.

2. Waterfalls
The island is full of waterfalls worth visiting, including Sendang Gile, Tiu Kelep, Benang Stokel, Benang Kelambu, and Jelantik. They are a great way to cool down after a day in the heat.

3. Beaches
Lombok's beaches are among the island's biggest highlights. Kuta Lombok, Tanjung Aan, Selong Belanak, and Pink Beach offer soft sand, clear water, and calm spots for swimming, surfing, or watching the sunset.

4. Transport
The easiest way to explore Lombok is by motorbike or by hiring a private driver. In some towns you can also use ride-hailing apps like Grab or Gojek, but public transport is limited.

5. Best time to visit
The dry season is usually the best time for travel, especially if you want to hike or spend more time outdoors. The wet season can still be beautiful, but roads and trails may be more challenging.

6. Local food
Try Ayam Taliwang, Plecing Kangkung, Sate Tanjung, and Nasi Balap. Lombok's cuisine is bold, spicy, and one of the best ways to understand the island's character.

7. Hidden corners
For something quieter, visit Sukarara Village, Gili Sudak, or Pusuk Pass. These places are perfect if you want local crafts, relaxed island views, and a slower pace.

8. Customs and culture
Lombok is rich in tradition. Pura Meru, Peresean performances, and Cakranegara Market all show different sides of local life and culture.

Lombok is easy to love: it combines mountains, beaches, food, and local culture in one trip. If you want an island adventure with a calmer rhythm than Bali, Lombok is a strong choice.
